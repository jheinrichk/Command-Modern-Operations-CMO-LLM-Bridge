# Scenario Gallery

[Back to overview](../README.md) · [Using CONN](CONN.md)

The following are published **Command: Modern Operations** works by **jheinrichk**, developed using an LLM-assisted scenario-design and scripting approach. They illustrate the types of complex events, decisions, testing and Lua authoring supported by CONN.

The scenarios are separate Steam Workshop downloads. **You do not need CMO LLM Bridge to play them.**

## [Orbittoblast](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3830546106)

*Objects in Orbit Are Closer Than They Appear.*

A near-future orbital escalation scenario in which attacks on space assets and the resulting debris have consequences for communications, surveillance and military operations below. The player navigates intertwined military and political decisions.

## [Election Day Surprise](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3827337947)

A fictional Middle East crisis unfolding alongside election politics. Scripted decisions and alternative escalation paths put political judgment alongside air and missile operations.

## [Surovikin's Return: The Fortress Belt](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3813282207)

A four-part Donbas campaign featuring air and ground operations, drones, electronic warfare, and decisions that carry across chapters.

[Matrix Games forum discussion](https://forums.matrixgames.com/viewtopic.php?t=418897)

## [Hormuz 2026: The Southern Route](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3808807124)

A Strait of Hormuz convoy scenario involving mines, escort operations, regional tensions and choices about escalation. Scripted events and scoring make restraint as relevant as combat effectiveness.

[Matrix Games forum discussion](https://forums.matrixgames.com/viewtopic.php?t=418898)

---

The Steam Workshop pages contain the authoritative current downloads, scenario instructions and version requirements. All four scenarios depict fictional events and are independent of the CMO Bridge application.
