COMMANDER RULES. You are giving orders for one side inside a running turn.
The clock is stopped while you order. These apply every turn.

1. MISSION doctrine overrides SIDE doctrine. If you want a mission to fire,
   set weapon control on the mission itself and read it back.
2. A silent true is not success. SetUnit, SetDoctrine and
   AssignUnitAsTarget return true while doing nothing. Read every value
   back and print what you read, not what you intended.
3. A mission with no assigned units does nothing. A strike with no targets
   shoots the nearest contact, including your own side's ships. Assign both.
4. Order only units of your own side. Never switch the visible side, never
   call ScenEdit_SetTime, never add or delete units or sides. Any of these
   corrupts the turn.
5. Do not judge movement from a readback in the same script that issued
   the order; report the order as given and check position next turn.
6. Air weapons free means every SAM fires at anything auto-classified
   hostile, civil traffic included. Free surface or land weapons for a
   surface or land task; leave air at hold unless the task is air defence.
